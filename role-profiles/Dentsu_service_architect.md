# Dentsu Aegis Network

I earned my promotion to Service Architect at a pivotal moment in Dentsu's technology transformation journey. The organisation was rapidly evolving, bringing critical services back in-house while simultaneously building new digital capabilities for a global business operating across many regions and brands.

My first major challenge was leading the modernisation of the company's ServiceNow platform. The platform was heavily customised, widely used across the business, and considered too important to fail. By bringing together the right people, focusing on outcomes, and maintaining a relentless attention to detail, I led the successful upgrade of the platform in just five weeks, delivering the project three weeks ahead of schedule while maintaining operational stability.

Beyond individual projects, I was responsible for helping shape the future direction of service technology across the organisation. Working closely with senior leadership, I developed a ServiceNow product roadmap aligned to the strategic priorities of the Global CTO. This roadmap provided clarity, direction, and focus for multiple transformation initiatives while balancing the operational needs of teams across the business.

Recognising that successful transformation depends on people as much as technology, I helped establish a new Business Analysis capability. By recruiting and mentoring junior team members, I built a stronger pipeline of talent and enabled better engagement between technical teams and business stakeholders. Within weeks, these new team members were making valuable contributions to ongoing programmes.

As the organisation's Service Management authority within architecture discussions, I worked across a wide variety of initiatives to ensure systems were designed with operational excellence in mind. I published technology standards covering Service Management, Event Management, Monitoring, and Collaboration platforms, creating a consistent foundation that could be adopted globally.

What I enjoyed most about this role was helping teams see the bigger picture. Technology decisions are not just about systems and tools; they are about enabling people to work more effectively and helping organisations achieve their goals. By combining architecture, strategy, governance, and delivery, I was able to contribute to a transformation that extended far beyond any single platform.

**Skills gained:** Service Architecture, ServiceNow, Technology Strategy, Enterprise Governance, Team Leadership, Business Analysis, Service Management, Platform Modernisation.
