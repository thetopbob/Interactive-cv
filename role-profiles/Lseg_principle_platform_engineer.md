# London Stock Exchange Group

When I joined the London Stock Exchange Group, I was asked to help transform a collection of critical platforms that sat at the heart of the business.

By introducing a modern approach to engineering and change delivery, I helped teams deploy faster and more frequently, enabling more than seventy successful releases each year across a highly complex technology landscape. This was achieved without compromising the reliability expected of a global financial institution.

Recognising that strong internal capability is just as important as great technology, I led the insourcing of key DevOps and Quality Engineering functions, helping build a sustainable foundation for future growth. At the same time, I reviewed the tooling ecosystem, removing unnecessary complexity and delivering more than £500,000 in annual savings.

These achievements ultimately led to my selection for the Global Enterprise Engineers programme, recognising my contribution to platform engineering, strategic transformation, and technical leadership.
