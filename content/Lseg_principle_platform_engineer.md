# London Stock Exchange Group

At LSEG, my mission evolved from transforming DevOps practices into building and leading engineering capabilities at enterprise scale.

I joined following a successful consulting engagement, taking on responsibility for transforming the Salesforce engineering capability supporting the Sales & Service platforms underpinning a £4bn revenue engine. The challenge was to dramatically increase the organisation's ability to deliver change while maintaining the stability expected of a highly critical enterprise platform.

The first quest was release engineering. The existing capability supported around 180 developers but was delivering only 12 releases a year. I transformed the operating model and engineering capability, increasing this to more than 70 releases annually while maintaining product stability.

Building the right team was central to the transformation. I recruited and established an in-house DevOps capability of 14 engineers, bringing critical expertise into the organisation and creating the foundations for sustainable engineering delivery.

My remit then expanded into Quality Engineering, where I led a 50-person capability supporting 20 engineering squads through a team of managers. I helped drive automated test coverage from 70% to more than 85%, strengthening quality and confidence across increasingly frequent releases.

As LSEG pivoted to wider adoption of AI-assisted development, I saw opportunities to embed AI into development cycles of our teams while maintaining close engineering oversight, code review and quality controls, one resource-constrained squad was able to reduce features that previously took months to build down to days.

As the transformation progressed, my role expanded beyond Salesforce into cross-platform engineering. I began leading capability across AWS and other emerging platforms, including a new team of three engineers working on platforms being built from the ground up.

I remained deliberately hands-on throughout this growth, continuing to contribute to technical and architectural decisions and staying close to the technology while leading increasingly large and complex engineering capabilities.

The transformation also created significant commercial value. By identifying opportunities to build strategic capabilities in-house rather than purchase commercial products, I delivered more than £500k in annual cost savings while aligning investment with the wider engineering strategy.

My impact was recognised through selection for LSEG's Global Enterprise Engineering programme, chosen from hundreds of applicants to join a small cohort focused on enterprise-level engineering leadership. I have also consistently received the highest available performance rating and been recognised as a top contributor throughout my tenure.

**Skills gained**: Engineering Leadership, Team Building, Recruitment, Platform Engineering, Product Engineering, DevOps, Quality Engineering, Salesforce, AWS, AI-Assisted Development, Engineering Strategy, Technical Leadership, Cost Optimisation, Organisational Transformation.
