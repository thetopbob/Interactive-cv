# Harrods

When I joined Harrods, the organisation was embarking on an ambitious transformation programme. A major objective was replacing an ageing and costly eCommerce platform while modernising the business capabilities that supported it.

My role was to ensure that new technology could be introduced successfully without disrupting the exceptional customer experience that the Harrods brand is known for. To achieve this, I established a Service Design and Transition framework that provided structure, consistency, and confidence during a period of rapid change.

One of my most significant contributions was designing the service model for the new eCommerce capability. This involved defining processes, selecting Service Management approaches, and creating clear interactions between internal teams and strategic delivery partners. By building these foundations early, the organisation was able to scale delivery while maintaining operational control.

As the transformation accelerated, I identified opportunities to automate manual processes that were slowing teams down. Improvements to Change and Incident communications reduced administrative effort and enabled faster, more reliable collaboration between operations and development teams.

I also helped guide several high-profile service introductions. A notable example was the implementation of Workday, where I designed an Early Life Support model that brought together colleagues from IT, Payroll, and HR. This ensured issues could be identified and resolved quickly during the critical launch period.

Recognising the growing demand for governance and service introduction expertise, I recruited and led a Service Design and Transition team. Together we supported more than fifty live projects, helping ensure that change was introduced in a controlled and sustainable manner.

Towards the end of my tenure, I project managed key aspects of the migration to the new eCommerce platform. This included defining cutover plans and establishing a cross-functional Command Centre that coordinated activities across multiple teams, ensuring a smooth transition to the new environment.

Looking back, this role strengthened my ability to balance technology, people, and process during large-scale transformation programmes. It demonstrated how effective service design can enable organisations to move quickly while maintaining stability and trust.

**Skills gained:** Service Design, Digital Transformation, eCommerce Platforms, Team Leadership, Process Automation, Service Introduction, ITIL, Programme Delivery, Change Management.
